Startup landscape II — retrieval, data stores, observability and agent tooling

The layers around the context pipeline are funded, crowded and consolidating: retrieval platforms compete on price, vector stores are becoming a data type inside incumbent databases, observability has two unicorn-scale leaders, and gateways are being acquired. Nearly all now market a "memory" or "context" feature. The open seat is measuring context quality, not another store.

Retrieval platforms: price competition and the "context engine" reframe

Glean is the scaled incumbent for feeding enterprise context to agents: Sacra estimates $300M ARR in May 2026, up from $208M at end-2025. Below it, Contextual AI (about $100M raised; HSBC and Qualcomm as early customers, sweep snippet, unverified) and Vectara ($73.5M) sell grounded RAG to regulated buyers, and integrator buyer guides now lead with compliance (Sphere). Pure managed RAG is already price-compressed: Ragie publishes $100–500/month tiers and reportedly poaches Vectara customers with discounted onboarding (Forage). Vectara open-sourced a RAG evaluation framework in April 2025 (sweep snippet, unverified) — a retrieval vendor moving into measurement. Onyx ($10M seed, 40+ connectors, open source) challenges Glean; LlamaIndex's capital goes to ingestion (LlamaParse), backed by 3M+ monthly downloads and 38,000 GitHub stars (sweep snippet, unverified).

Web data for agents is its own commoditising sub-market: Exa, Tavily and Firecrawl are benchmarked head-to-head (AIMultiple, eight APIs), and Tavily was reportedly acquired by Nebius in February 2026 (snippet, unverified). RAGFlow's year-end review captures the positioning shift: RAG is being reframed as "a Context Engine with intelligent retrieval as its core", with tool retrieval and memory over interaction logs named as new retrieval problems, and GraphRAG flagged for consuming "several to dozens of times more tokens than original text" (RAGFlow). Every retrieval vendor now calls itself a context platform.

Vector and graph stores: a data type, not a category

Four research houses put the vector-database market at $2.0–3.2B (2025/26; syndicated estimate, weak methodology) with 22–28% CAGRs (MarketsandMarkets $2.65B; GMI $2.55B; Research and Markets $3.02B), and MarketsandMarkets ranks Microsoft, MongoDB, Elastic, Google and AWS above the pure plays. VentureBeat's 2026 read is that vectors became a data type inside Oracle, Google databases and S3, PostgreSQL became the default for GenAI, and purpose-built vector-DB use cases will narrow. Incumbent M&A agrees: MongoDB bought Voyage AI (Feb 2025), Databricks bought Neon ($1B), Snowflake bought Crunchy Data ($250M) (VentureBeat).

turbopuffer is the counter-example. It keeps data in object storage with an NVMe cache, prices around $0.02/GB against RAM-era architectures, and reached roughly $100M annualised revenue in March 2026 (from $75M at end-2025) having raised under $1M of primary capital. Its largest customer is Cursor ("over a trillion chunks of code across more than 80 million namespaces"); Notion migrated 10B+ vectors in October 2024 and then dropped per-user AI charges (Sacra). The store that won inside coding agents sold economics, not features. Memory is the feature every store is adding: Sacra's Mem0 profile lists Pinecone, Weaviate, Chroma and Qdrant as "expanding into memory", Weaviate's cloud plans bundle "Agents" (sweep snippet, unverified), Zep's Graphiti temporal graph (28,000+ stars) is the graph-store route, and Cloudflare is listed as a memory provider alongside the startups (Developers Digest).

Observability, evals and gateways: consolidation and a measurement gap

Braintrust's $80M Series B at $800M post-money (Iconiq, Feb 2026; customers include Notion, Replit, Cloudflare, Ramp) is the clearest context-observability bet; its tracing records "prompts, tool calls, retrieved context and metadata on latency and cost" per agent step. LangSmith's monthly trace volume grew 12x year over year; its 2026 cost view spans retrieval, tools and external APIs. Langfuse, the MIT-licensed self-host leader, is now part of ClickHouse; Arize Phoenix (Elastic License 2.0) reports two million monthly downloads (sweep snippet, unverified). The gap: none of the platforms compared offers dedicated context-window or context-rot tracking — token counts per span are the norm (MarkTechPost).

Gateways hold caching and routing, and they are being absorbed. OpenRouter raised $113M in May 2026 with Google and NVIDIA venture arms, a year after its June 2025 Series A at $547M post-money (TechCrunch); weekly token volume reportedly grew from 5T to 25T in six months (Tech Times; page not fetchable). Portkey starts at $49/month; a snippet we could not confirm reports a Palo Alto Networks acquisition announced April 2026. LiteLLM stays open source with no disclosed round; Vercel and Cloudflare ship their own gateways. Gateway-level prompt caching is claimed to save 40–60% on repeated patterns (Requesty), the cheapest context optimisation a buyer can switch on — and it belongs to the gateway.

Frameworks and coding agents

LangChain ($125M at $1.25B; 90M combined monthly downloads for langchain and langgraph; 35% of the Fortune 500) positions LangGraph on durable execution with "memory and human-in-the-loop", and LangMem is listed as the framework-native memory alternative in vendor comparisons. LlamaIndex ships its own Memory module. Mem0's strategy is to be the default memory inside those frameworks — 25+ integrations, an MCP server, exclusive memory provider for AWS's Strands SDK — so frameworks are both channel and competitor for any memory-layer entrant (Mem0).

Coding agents are the heaviest consumers of context infrastructure, and retrieval choices diverge most there: Cursor's index lives in turbopuffer; Claude Code went index-free with grep-based retrieval (cited by RAGFlow); Letta moved its front door to Letta Code in 2026, claiming 42.5% on Terminal-Bench (Ry Walker). A local-first, "forever free" memory for Claude users launched on Hacker News against cloud memory, marking a privacy segment. Cursor/Anysphere funding is not in this section's evidence base and is not estimated.

Implications for a new entrant

  • Do not build a store or a gateway: vectors are becoming a data type in incumbent databases, and gateway leaders were bought or platformised within twelve months.
  • The measurable gap is context quality — what entered the window, whether it changed the answer, how it degraded over turns — which no observability platform tracks; ship it as a plug-in to Braintrust, LangSmith and Langfuse traces, not a new tracing product.
  • Treat LangChain, LlamaIndex and the vector DBs as channels first: Mem0's 25-integration and AWS Strands strategy shows distribution decides the memory layer, and each framework already ships a native memory module.
  • Copy turbopuffer's go-to-market, not its product: one anchor coding-agent customer and a 10x cost advantage produced ~$100M revenue on under $1M raised.
  • If you must sell retrieval, sell compliance in a regulated vertical; horizontal managed RAG already discounts to poach customers.
  • Write-path memory without LLM calls attracts new seed entrants monthly; enter only with a published latency and cost benchmark against Mem0, Zep and Letta.
