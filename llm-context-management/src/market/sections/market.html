Market structure, size and money

The money in context management sits at the edges of the value chain, not in the middle. Model APIs took $12.5B of 2025 enterprise GenAI spend and applications $19B; all non-model, non-training infrastructure (vector DBs, gateways, memory, observability) combined took $1.5B. Six funded memory-layer startups (seven profiled, Memobase undisclosed) have raised about $51M between them and none discloses revenue. Bottom-up evidence says the layer is real but early; the analyst TAMs say otherwise and should be discounted.

The value chain and where the margin sits

Five layers, with revenue concentrated at the top and bottom.

  • Model vendors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta). Foundation-model APIs took $12.5B of 2025 enterprise spend (Menlo 2025). Vendors are moving down into context: OpenAI bought Rockset in June 2024 (Synthedia); Claude Code added auto-memory in late 2025 (Zylos).
  • Serving and infrastructure. Gateways (OpenRouter, Portkey, LiteLLM, Vercel) and embedding storage. OpenRouter's weekly tokens reportedly grew from 5T to 25T in the six months to May 2026 (Tech Times; not fetched). Storage is being absorbed by incumbents: MarketsandMarkets names Microsoft, MongoDB and Elastic the "star players"; MongoDB bought Voyage AI in February 2025 (SolvedByCode); vectors became a data type inside Oracle, Google and S3 (VentureBeat).
  • Context and memory layer (the subject of this report). Memory APIs: Mem0, Letta, Zep/Graphiti, Cognee, Supermemory, Plastic Labs' Honcho, Hindsight. Retrieval platforms: Contextual AI, Vectara, Ragie. Ingestion and web data: LlamaCloud, Unstructured, Firecrawl, Exa, Tavily.
  • Frameworks and tooling. LangChain/LangGraph: 90M monthly downloads, 35% of the Fortune 500 (LangChain); LlamaIndex: 3M monthly downloads (PR Newswire). Observability vendors (Braintrust, LangSmith, Arize, Langfuse) capture "prompts, tool calls, retrieved context" per agent step (SiliconANGLE).
  • Applications. $19B of 2025 spend (Menlo). Glean (~$300M ARR by May 2026 per Sacra) and Cursor are applications whose core product is context assembly; Cursor buys storage from turbopuffer, not a memory layer (Sacra).

Three problems with the syndicated numbers. Definitions: a $2.0–3.2B vector-database market exceeds Menlo's entire $1.5B non-model, non-training infrastructure bucket, which only works if incumbent database revenue is being counted as "vector". Players: SkyQuest names IBM, Palantir, UiPath and Darktrace and no memory startup, so it measures automation platforms, not the layer a startup would enter. Dispersion: out-year forecasts differ about 2x and the agent umbrella spans $139B to $295B. Use them for direction, not a TAM slide.

Bottom-up signals: revenue floors, usage, demand

Revenue floors. turbopuffer, the object-storage search engine under Cursor's codebase index, reached about $100M annualized revenue in March 2026 from $75M at end-2025, on under $1M of primary capital (Sacra). Glean is estimated at $300M ARR in May 2026 (Sacra). Contextual AI, after ~$100M raised, is estimated at $10.2M ARR (Latka, low confidence). Scaled revenue sits in storage and enterprise retrieval, not memory APIs.

Usage without revenue. Mem0's API calls grew from 35M in Q1 2025 to 186M in Q3 2025, with 80,000+ cloud signups (TechCrunch); Sacra records no ARR for it (Sacra). Cognee's pipeline runs went from ~2,000 to over 1M in 2025 across 70+ companies (Cognee). GitHub stars on July 20, 2026: Mem0 61,323; Graphiti 28,981; Cognee 28,786; Supermemory 28,509; Letta 23,887 (Mnemoverse). Self-serve prices are small: Mem0 up to $249/month, Letta $20/month, Zep $1,250/year.

Deriving the pool (illustration, not a sourced figure): if Mem0 converted 5% of its 80,000 signups to the $249/month tier, that is about $12M ARR, consistent with a $20M Series A. Across six funded vendors, dedicated memory-API revenue in 2025 was very likely under $50M.

Demand side. RAG was in 51% of enterprise AI implementations in 2024, up from 31% (Menlo 2024, via MarketsandMarkets), but only 16% of enterprise deployments are "true agents" and context engineering is "niche, used primarily by frontier teams" (Menlo 2025). Only about $750M of the $19B application spend went to agentic platforms (No Jitter). CIOs expect LLM budgets up ~75% and 37% already run five or more models (a16z).

Investors pay for the trace, the bytes and the search seat, not yet for memory alone.

What investors are betting on

  • Memory is the missing agent primitive. Pebblebed's Pamela Vagata on leading Cognee: "Agents need real memory to be real products" (Cognee); VentureBeat predicts memory becomes "table stakes" for operational agents in 2026 (VentureBeat).
  • A neutral layer gains value as model use fragments. 37% of CIOs run five or more models and switching costs rise with agentic workflows (a16z); Mem0's "open, neutral memory" pitch and its exclusive slot in AWS's Agent SDK express this (TechCrunch).
  • Context as the new system of record. a16z's Big Ideas 2026: "the system of record will finally start to lose primacy" (LinkedIn summary, secondary).
  • Observability is the monetisation point. Braintrust at $800M, LangSmith traces up 12x, Langfuse bought by ClickHouse: where context failures become visible, budgets already exist.
  • Counter-thesis: economics and incumbents. turbopuffer reached ~$100M ARR at roughly $0.02/GB on object storage against $3,600/TB/month RAM-era systems (Sacra); vectors are being absorbed into Postgres, MongoDB and Elastic.
  • Reality check. Context engineering is niche and only 16% of enterprise deployments are true agents (Menlo); the thesis runs one to two years ahead of the spend.

Implications for a new entrant

  • Do not size the company on the $6B "orchestration and memory" TAM; the pool for dedicated memory APIs was probably under $50M in 2025, so a seed plan must assume category creation, not share capture.
  • Capital flows to whoever owns the trace (observability) or the bytes (storage); attach a memory product to one of those rather than selling it standalone.
  • Distribution beats features: Mem0's AWS Agent SDK exclusivity shows that being the default inside a framework or cloud SDK is the channel; negotiate one before the Series A.
  • Model vendors will bundle basic memory; position on what they will not do: cross-vendor portability, temporal facts (Zep), regulated-industry deployment (Cognee at Bayer), audit and evaluation.
  • Stars do not convert by themselves: Mem0 has 61,000 stars and no disclosed ARR, while LangChain needed LangSmith to reach $1.25B, so design the paid surface from day one.
  • Sell to the 16% of deployments that are true agents (coding and support agents), where context failures already cost money, rather than to the prompt-and-RAG majority.
