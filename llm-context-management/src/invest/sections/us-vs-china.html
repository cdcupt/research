US vs China: the competitive investment picture

Same AI boom, two capital systems. The US runs on private mega-rounds: OpenAI and Anthropic took $217B — 43% of all H1 2026 global venture funding — and Anthropic priced at $965B. China runs state-anchored and public-market-first: Zhipu and MiniMax listed in Hong Kong in January 2026, Moonshot hit $35B, DeepSeek's $7.4B round reportedly reserves voting rights for the state, and a reported $295B national plan substitutes for private capex.

Two valuation ladders, an order of magnitude apart

The US ladder is unprecedented in private-market history: Anthropic closed a $65B Series H at $965B post-money on May 28, 2026 (verified on Anthropic's own announcement), and by Aug 14 traded as high as $1.5T on secondary markets per Business Insider (fetch blocked; per headline). OpenAI closed a record $122B round on Mar 31, 2026 — confirmed by Crunchbase — at an $852B valuation reported by SiliconANGLE (per headline). xAI closed $20B on Jan 6, 2026, above its $15B target. Even Safe Superintelligence — roughly 50 employees, no product — carried $32B (Yahoo Finance, Jul 29).

China's ladder tops out at roughly 1/25th of that. Moonshot AI (月之暗面) oversubscribed a $3.5B round to reach $35B on Jul 29, 2026 (Bloomberg, corroborated by Silicon Republic, Unite.AI and The Standard the same day, all per headline), opened a pre-IPO round at ~$50B pre-money on Aug 5 (财联社, per headline), and completed joint-stock reform (股改) with Yang Zhilin (杨植麟) as chairman on Aug 21 ahead of a Hong Kong IPO (新浪财经, per headline). DeepSeek's first external round closed at ~$50B ($59B in some reports, all per headline). Velocity, however, matches the US: 21财经 (verified) tracks Moonshot from $4.3B (Dec 2025) to over $20B (May 7, 2026) — 8x in eight months — on more than $3.9B raised across four rounds in under six months.

Who writes the checks: concentrated VC vs state capital

US capital is private but extraordinarily concentrated. Crunchbase (verified): H1 2026 global venture funding hit a record $510B — above all of 2025 — with OpenAI and Anthropic alone at $217B, 43% of everything. In Q1, AI took 80% of global venture funding and four US companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Waymo) took ~65% of the world's total. Strategics sit inside the rounds: $15B of Anthropic's Series H came from hyperscalers (including $5B from Amazon), Nvidia and Cisco invested in xAI, and Nvidia put $5B into SSI (Bloomberg, Jul 27, per headline).

China's stack replaced dollar VC with the state. USD-fund activity in China's primary market fell 84% over five years (新浪财经, Jan 8, per headline). In its place: the national IC Big Fund (大基金) in talks to lead DeepSeek at ~$45B (verified in 21财经's May 7 report as under negotiation), closing as a $7.4B round in which only the state investor received voting rights (Trending Topics/Tech Times/Forbes, Jun 16-17, per headlines); Shanghai state capital in MiniMax's pre-IPO C round (财联社, verified); China Mobile and CPE alongside Meituan Longzhu in Moonshot's May round (21财经, verified). Above it all sits Beijing's $295B nationwide AI-buildout plan (Bloomberg via Reuters, Jun 9, per headline), reportedly requiring ~80% domestic chips (The Decoder, Jun 9, per headline). The walls are legal in both directions: reverse-CFIUS outbound screening was codified in Jan 2026 (Skadden, per headline), and Beijing tightened retail access to US stocks in June (CNBC, per headline).

Public-market access is inverted

China listed first. Zhipu debuted Jan 7, 2026 after a $560M raise; MiniMax more than doubled on debut Jan 9 (CNBC, per headlines). By Jun 30, 华尔街见闻 (verified) put Zhipu's market cap at ~¥1 trillion — about 20x its listing price — against just ¥724M of 2025 revenue (MaaS ARR ~¥1.7B), a four-digit trailing multiple the same article contrasts with OpenAI's ~35x forward multiple. The correction leg is equally real: MiniMax lost ~$39B of market cap into July's record lock-up expiries (Forbes, Jul 6, per headline), down over 60% from a peak above HK$400B, and Alibaba's US$10.2B (HK$80B) AI-funding placement (priced Aug 23–24) knocked the Hang Seng below 25,500 on Aug 24 (finance.biggo, per headline).

No US frontier lab is public. Anthropic filed in June 2026 with a debut expected within months and ~$2T valuation talk (Business Insider/Fortune, Aug 14, per headlines); PitchBook data (verified) counts only 15 VC-backed IPOs in Q1 2026 and warns that OpenAI+Anthropic+SpaceX (~$3T combined) could absorb available IPO capital and push the broader liquidity recovery to 2027. The aggregate scoreboard is stark: Chinese stocks trailed US peers in the AI boom by the most since 2001 as of end-June (Bloomberg/NDTV Profit, per headline) — even as individual HK AI names ran hundreds of percent.

Open weights and chip constraints as investment strategy

The chip wall is now total: a US Commerce official confirmed Nvidia H200 China sales at zero with approvals stalled (电子工程专辑, Feb 25, per headline); Chinese media describe Nvidia's China AI-chip share going from ~95% to ~0% (36Kr, May 8, per headline). The investable mirror image is domestic substitution — Cambricon (寒武纪) peaked at a ¥1T (~$140B) market cap on Jun 30, 2026, then fell below ¥700B (~$97B) by Aug 13, 2026, and guided to $14B revenue over three years (SCMP, Jul 29, per headlines) — and model-level hedging: DeepSeek V4 shipped open weights with day-0 Huawei Ascend support (InfoQ, Apr 24, per headline).

Open weights are China's market-entry weapon where closed APIs face restrictions: Kimi K3 (2.8T-param MoE, 1M context), GLM-5.2 (#1 globally available on Code Arena, Jun 18, per headline) and MiniMax M3 are all open. The result reaches US investors anyway — "Wall Street is betting on Zhipu as Washington's export crackdown hands Chinese models a gift" (Startup Fortune, Jun 17, per headline) — even with Zhipu on the Entity List. US labs keep weights closed and sell run-rate: Anthropic's went from $47B (May 28, verified) to over $65B (Reuters, Aug 17, per headline).

What each side's investors expect

US investors underwrite revenue velocity and capex payoff — JPMorgan raised its S&P 500 target on Aug 10 because "AI capex is finally delivering"; Citi targets 8,100 (Jun 8) — while the bear chorus grows (Norway's sovereign fund, Aug 18; ECB economists, Aug 19; BofA's bubble gauge near dot-com extremes, Aug 7; Bill Dudley calling a burst by end-2027, Aug 24 — all per headlines). Q1 Series A AI deals priced 84% above non-AI peers (PitchBook, verified). Chinese investors pay for 确定性 (certainty) and listing proximity: ~¥300B of hot money entered the AI primary market by Aug 19 (or100.cc, Aug 19, 2026, per headline), A/H dual listings recycle the pop, and the state anchors downside — but 2025 revenues of ¥724M (Zhipu) and ~¥560M (MiniMax, per headline) mean the multiple, not the P&L, carries the story through each lock-up cliff.

Implications

  • The risk barbell differs by side: US risk is multiple compression on ~$1T private marks with scarce exits (15 VC-backed IPOs in Q1 2026); China risk is post-lockup collapse on triple-digit trailing multiples (MiniMax −$39B). Size positions to the failure mode, not the theme.
  • With $217B (43%) of H1 2026 global VC in two US names, US venture returns are hostage to the Anthropic/OpenAI listing calendar; PitchBook's 2027-slip scenario is the single biggest liquidity variable.
  • State-led rounds change governance math: DeepSeek's sole-state voting rights mean minority investors buy exposure, not influence — apply a governance discount to Chinese AI privates with state anchors.
  • Chip walls create paired trades: long domestic substitution (Cambricon ¥1T peak Jun 30 → below ¥700B by Aug 13, 2026, Ascend supply chain, the 80%-domestic-chip mandate) is the mirror of shrinking US China-exposure (Super Micro −33% on smuggling charges, Mar 19).
  • Every open-weight flagship (K3, GLM-5.2, M3, V4) is also a pricing event for closed-model margins worldwide; watch API price cuts as the leading indicator of margin pressure on US labs.
  • Hong Kong is the release valve for Chinese AI capital: Moonshot's IPO (股改 completed Aug 21, $35-50B range) is the next re-rating event for the whole cohort.
