Addendum: China Memory Vendor Identity Check

Two identity questions behind the China vendor table are now resolved. Memories.ai is a US-domiciled company (ex-Meta founders, US lead investor, US counsel) and should not be counted as a China-market memory vendor. The unnamed Hao Jianye (郝建业) startup is MemoraX AI, a Shenzhen "endogenous memory" (内生记忆) company that closed three rounds in six months: a $10M+ seed (Apr 2026) and a several-hundred-million-RMB seed++ (Aug 2026); the middle round's size is unknown.

Key numbersMemories.ai: $8M seed (Jul 24, 2025), $16M total after an $8M extension (as of Mar 16, 2026), 15 employees (Jul 2025). MemoraX AI: 千万美元-level ($10M+) seed (Apr 28, 2026), 数亿元 RMB seed++ (Aug 11, 2026), 3 rounds in ~6 months (36Kr, Aug 11, 2026).

Memories.ai: US-domiciled, not a China-market player

Memories.ai was founded by Dr. Shawn Shen, a former Meta Reality Labs research scientist with a Cambridge PhD, and Enmin (Ben) Zhou, a former Meta machine-learning engineer. Its $8M seed (announced Jul 24, 2025; oversubscribed from a $4M target; 15 employees at the time) was led by Susa Ventures, joined by Samsung Next, Fusion Fund, Crane Ventures, Seedcamp and Creator Ventures, per TechCrunch. By Mar 16, 2026 the company had raised $16M total across the seed and an $8M extension (also reported by Dealroom, per headline, Jun 23, 2026), and was shipping a Large Visual Memory Model with Qualcomm and Nvidia partnerships and the LUCI wearable, per TechCrunch.

No fetched source names an HQ city — the company site says only that the work "began at Cambridge" — but three independent signals point to US domicile: Tech in Asia headlined the round "Samsung backs $8m seed round of US ex-Meta team AI startup" (per headline, Jul 25, 2025); US law firm Wilson Sonsini acted as company counsel on the seed (per headline, Jul 24, 2025); and the cap table is US/EU/Korean-corporate with no Chinese investor reported. Founders of Chinese origin and Chinese-press coverage do not make it a China-market vendor: it sells through Qualcomm/Qualcomm-class OEM channels, not into the China stack.

MemoraX AI: the Hao Jianye company, named and dated

The mystery company is MemoraX AI, based in Shenzhen (深圳). Chinese tech press identifies the founder as an ex-Huawei senior figure ("华为大牛" / "前华为高管"), and 36Kr names him directly: "给大模型做记忆，郝建业半年融三轮" — Hao Jianye (郝建业), building memory for large models, three rounds in half a year (per headline, Aug 11, 2026). The company's positioning is "endogenous memory" (内生记忆) for large models, per 品玩 (PingWest) (per headline, Apr 29, 2026). Its official sites — memorax.ai ("the memory layer for long-horizon intelligence") and memorax.net (reinforcement-learning-based memory; per Bing snippets, Aug 24, 2026) — match Hao's reinforcement-learning profile.

What remains unverified

All MemoraX figures above are headline-level: 36Kr, 智东西, 投资界, 品玩 and Sina article bodies sit behind bot walls and Google News redirect throttling, so exact round sizes, the seed++ investor list, and the seed+ round are unconfirmed. Hao Jianye's widely assumed Tianjin University professorship and Huawei Noah's Ark background appear in no fetched source — Chinese headlines say only "前华为高管". For Memories.ai, the HQ city (San Jose vs. Seattle vs. other) is unknown; US domicile rests on the Tech in Asia label, US counsel, and the cap table rather than a registry document.

Implications

  • Remove Memories.ai from the China vendor table, or reclassify it as "US company, Chinese-origin founders" — the "at least four venture-backed China memory startups" count must be re-based without it.
  • MemoraX AI can anchor a genuine China slot: Shenzhen domicile, named Chinese investors (光源, 钟鼎), and three rounds in six months (Apr–Aug 2026) is the strongest China memory-funding datapoint of 2026 so far.
  • State MemoraX round sizes as ranges ("$10M+ seed", "数亿元 RMB seed++"), never as point figures — no exact number has a primary source yet.
  • The two firms illustrate divergent go-to-market: MemoraX raises domestic RMB and USD from Chinese funds; Memories.ai ($16M total, Mar 2026) monetizes via US chip-vendor partnerships (Qualcomm, Nvidia) — they do not compete in the same market today.
  • If a fourth China vendor is needed for the count, verify domicile via a corporate registry (天眼查-class) before inclusion — this addendum shows press coverage alone misclassifies domicile.
