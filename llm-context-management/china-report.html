The Chinese context-management stack

China's labs treat long context as an economics problem and publish the solution at every layer: attention compression (DeepSeek MLA→NSA→DSA→CSA/HCA), hybrid linear architectures (Qwen, Kimi, MiniMax), KV-cache-centric serving (Mooncake, now inside vLLM and SGLang), and a VC-funded memory-OS industry (MemOS). Because the flagship weights ship MIT/Apache, this stack is the substrate most open context research now builds on.

DeepSeek: attention compression as a pricing weapon

The through-line of DeepSeek's four attention generations is that each one converts a KV-cache saving into a public price cut. DeepSeek-V2 (May 2024, 236B/21B active, 128K context) introduced Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA), compressing the KV cache into a latent vector: 93.3% smaller cache and 5.76x higher max generation throughput than DeepSeek 67B — the same numbers anchored in the deep dive. Native Sparse Attention (Feb 2025) made sparsity trainable end-to-end and hardware-aligned; V3.2-Exp productionized it as DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) on Sep 29, 2025, benchmarking on par with V3.1-Terminus while API prices dropped 50%+ effective immediately.

The V4 report (posted Jun 2026, per its arXiv ID 2606.19348; the Apr 24, 2026 InfoQ-CN headline below dates the launch earlier — an unresolved discrepancy) is the current end-state: hybrid Compressed Sparse Attention + Heavily Compressed Attention, two MoE sizes (V4-Pro 1.6T/49B active; V4-Flash 284B/13B), 1M-token context, 32T+ pretraining tokens. At 1M context, V4-Pro needs only 27% of V3.2's single-token inference FLOPs and 10% of its KV cache. The weights are MIT-licensed (per Wikipedia, accessed Aug 24, 2026), and Chinese coverage stressed first-time day-0 Huawei Ascend support alongside Nvidia (per InfoQ-CN headline, Apr 24, 2026). The pricing follow-through was again immediate: V4-Pro API permanently cut to a quarter of list (per Caixin headline, May 25, 2026).

Qwen: training-free scaling, hybrid linear attention, free platform memory

Alibaba's line started from serving, not architecture: Dual Chunk Attention (Feb 2024) decomposes attention into chunk-wise pieces so a model serves 100K+ tokens with no extra training. Qwen2.5-1M (Jan 2025) shipped the first open-weight 1M-token models (7B/14B) plus an open-sourced inference framework whose sparse attention and chunked prefill deliver a 3–7x prefill speedup at 1M tokens. Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B (Apache 2.0) is the architecture bet: 12 blocks of three Gated DeltaNet (linear) layers per one Gated Attention layer, 512-expert ultra-sparse MoE activating 10+1, 262,144 tokens native and ~1,010,000 via YaRN. By Aug 2026 the line had scaled to Qwen3.8-Max on Alibaba Cloud, marketed on context capacity (per CRN Asia headline, Aug 7, 2026), with NVIDIA itself publishing a GB300 NVL72 serving guide for Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B (per NVIDIA blog headline, Aug 12, 2026).

Downstream, Alibaba gives the memory layer away: Bailian (百炼) long-term memory offers auto-extracted memory nodes and manual memory variables with free storage, and memory-content tokens "currently not charged" (docs, accessed Aug 2026) — a direct squeeze on standalone memory vendors.

Moonshot: co-designing attention and the serving fabric

Moonshot attacked both ends. MoBA (Feb 18, 2025) routes each query to a learned subset of context blocks — MoE applied to attention — and was already serving Kimi's long-context traffic at publication, with open code. On the serving side, Mooncake (Jun 2024, with Tsinghua) disaggregates prefill from decode around a distributed KV-cache pool; it won FAST 2025 Best Paper and lets Kimi handle 75% more requests within SLOs. The repo (6.4k stars, Aug 24, 2026) is now shared infrastructure: integrated by vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, LMDeploy, LMCache and NIXL, running on NVIDIA, AMD, Huawei, AWS, Cambricon and Moore Threads silicon. Kimi K3 (Jul 27, 2026) stacks the model side: 2.8T-parameter MoE, 104B active (16 of 896 routed experts via Stable LatentMoE), 1M-token context on Kimi Delta Attention + Attention Residuals, ~2.5x scaling efficiency over K2. The paper itself concedes K3 trails Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol while beating every other model it evaluated — open weights one notch behind the closed frontier.

The memory-OS line and China's agent-memory research base

Above the models sits a distinctly Chinese layer: memory as an operating-system abstraction. The MemOS paper (Jul 2025; MemTensor 记忆张量 + SJTU collaborators) unifies plaintext, activation and parameter-level memory in a versioned MemCube unit. The Apache-2.0 repo reached 11.0k stars by Aug 24, 2026, self-reporting LoCoMo 88.83, LongMemEval 89.20 and 35.24% token savings vs commercial memory products, and shipped a DeepSeek Harness plugin on Aug 17, 2026. Commercially, MemTensor closed a ~RMB 100M (亿元) Pre-A in Jul 2026 reportedly backed by Huawei, Honor and SenseTime (per 新浪财经/品玩 headlines), selling a fully-local MemOS Lite for on-prem/信创 buyers with enterprise names like Trip.com, Haier and China Telecom on its site.

It is not alone: Prof. Hao Jianye's (郝建业, Tianjin University) learnable-memory (可学习记忆) startup closed three rounds in six months (per 36氪 headline, Aug 11, 2026; sizes unknown), and Shenzhen's MemoraX raised a $10M+ seed for "endogenous memory" (内生记忆), reaching cumulative 数亿元 by Aug 2026 (per 投资界/智东西 headlines). The research base skews the same way: the canonical agent-memory survey (Apr 2024) came from Renmin University's Gaoling School of AI with Huawei collaborators, and Chinese groups dominate the follow-on benchmark and taxonomy literature that products like MemOS cite.

Open weights as the substrate of global context research

The strategic effect is compounding. Every mechanism above — MLA, DSA, MoBA, DeltaNet hybrids, lightning attention — can be downloaded, ablated and reused under MIT or Apache terms, which is why Western efficiency papers benchmark against DeepSeek and Qwen rather than closed US frontier models (whose windows are discussed in the deep dive). The serving layer crossed over outright: Mooncake merged into vLLM, SGLang and TensorRT-LLM workflows, and NVIDIA now writes deployment guides for Qwen flagships. Day-0 Ascend support in V4 plus Mooncake's Huawei/Cambricon backends make the whole stack the first credible non-Nvidia long-context path.

Implications

  • Attention generations now arrive as price events: DSA cut DeepSeek's API 50%+ on launch day (Sep 29, 2025); V4-Pro went to a quarter of list in May 2026. Budget models should assume Chinese long-context inference keeps deflating.
  • KV-cache disaggregation is shared plumbing: Mooncake improvements propagate through vLLM/SGLang/TensorRT-LLM to every stack, including US ones — adopting it is an integration choice, not a rewrite.
  • Million-token experiments no longer require a frontier API contract: V4 (MIT), K3, GLM-5.2, MiniMax M3 and Qwen 1M models are all open-weight as of mid-2026.
  • Memory is China's next funded layer (three startups raised in 2026 alone), but platforms like Bailian price memory at zero — standalone vendors must win on portability and on-prem (信创) deployment, as MemOS Lite does.
  • Hardware bifurcation is real: day-0 Huawei Ascend support (V4) and multi-vendor Mooncake backends mean the Chinese context stack runs off-Nvidia; efficiency techniques tuned only for CUDA may not transfer back.
  • Caveat: open weights trail the closed frontier — Moonshot's own K3 paper places it behind Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol; the Chinese advantage is cost-per-token-of-context, not peak capability.

The Chinese market for context and memory

China now has a funded memory-software category — three domestic venture-backed memory startups plus diaspora Memories.ai by mid-2026, led by MemTensor's MemOS — but it lives inside a brutal squeeze: platform giants bundle the memory on-ramp at zero price (metered at production scale on Coze since Oct 15, 2025), open-weight models ship 1M-token contexts, and API list prices fell to a quarter. State capital sets funding terms, and the Manus block closed the arbitrage. For a US entrant, China is a signal, not a territory.

Memory-native vendors: a funded category since mid-2026

The flagship is Shanghai's MemTensor (记忆张量), the SJTU-affiliated company behind the MemOS paper (Jul 2025) — memory as an OS-managed resource via the MemCube abstraction. The open-source core stands at 11.0k stars (Apache 2.0, verified Aug 24 2026) and claims LoCoMo 88.83, LongMemEval 89.20 and 35.24% token savings versus commercial memory products. Commercially it sells MemOS Cloud (free tier: 50K memory-adds / 20K searches per month) plus a fully-local MemOS Lite and private enterprise deployment, and names Trip.com, Anker, Haier, China Telecom and Alibaba Cloud among partners. It closed a ~¥100M (亿元) Pre-A in July 2026 from Huawei, Honor and SenseTime (per 新浪财经 headline) — strategic CVC, not USD venture.

It is not alone. Tianjin University professor Hao Jianye (郝建业) raised three rounds in six months for a "learnable memory" (可学习记忆) startup (36氪, Aug 11 2026; sizes unknown). Shenzhen's MemoraX AI, founded by an ex-Huawei chief expert, took a $10M+ seed for "endogenous memory" (内生记忆) in April 2026, reaching several hundred million RMB cumulative by August (per 智东西 headlines). Memories.ai (Chinese founder, ex-Meta; diaspora — likely US-domiciled, domicile unverified) sells a visual-memory layer ($8M seed Jul 2025, Samsung Next follow-on Feb 2026, per headlines). Even mainstream enterprise vendors adopted the framing: Deepexi's (滴普科技) CEO calls memory "the soul of the agent" (36Kr, May 7 2026).

Platforms price the memory on-ramp at zero

The squeeze on those startups is explicit and verifiable. Alibaba Cloud Bailian's long-term memory docs state memory storage is not charged (数据存储不收费) and tokens occupied by memory content are "currently not billed" (暂不计费), with indefinite retention (verified Aug 24 2026). ByteDance open-sourced Coze Studio — knowledge bases, databases, variables, conversation memory — under Apache 2.0, after commercial Coze had "served tens of thousands of enterprises." Hosted Coze memory, though, is free only as a 100-entry on-ramp: since an Oct 15, 2025 upgrade it is metered at 0.03 yuan per write, 0.01 yuan per retrieval and 0.02 yuan per 10k entries/hour (docs.coze.cn, Aug 24 2026) — ByteDance's free layer is the OpenViking repo, not hosted service. Dify gives away the full RAG pipeline self-hosted. Even MemTensor cannot hold price: its $286/month Pro tier is currently promoted at $0. In China, memory is a platform feature, not a product — a preview of the commoditization pressure everywhere else.

Funding: state capital and CVC set the terms

The financing backdrop is structurally different from the US. USD funds' activity in China's primary market fell 84% in five years (Sina Finance, Jan 2026), replaced by ~¥300B of state-linked "hot money" (or100.cc, Aug 19 2026) and a reported ~$295B national AI buildout plan (Bloomberg via Reuters, Jun 9 2026). The state semiconductor Big Fund (国家大基金) reportedly led DeepSeek's first round at $45B pre-money (华尔街见闻, May 28 2026); the round closed at ~$7.4B in June 2026 with only the state investor getting voting rights (per headlines; valuation reported $50–59B). Exits run through Hong Kong: Zhipu's $560M IPO listed Jan 7 2026 (first "AI tiger"), MiniMax doubled on debut then lost ~$39B of market cap in the July lock-up wave, and Moonshot hit $35B (Jul 29 2026) before opening a $50B pre-IPO round (财联社, Aug 5 2026). For the memory layer specifically, the pattern is CVC-at-seed: Huawei, Honor and SenseTime financing MemTensor means memory will be bundled into Chinese devices and OS stacks, not sold as neutral SaaS.

Enterprise requirements: xinchuang, dual filing, on-prem

Chinese enterprise demand is shaped by compliance, and it rewards local-first architectures. Government and SOE buyers procure under xinchuang (信创): iFlytek shipped a fully-domestic LLM appliance targeting party-government (党政) users (China Daily, May 11 2026), and vendor roundups make private deployment (可私有化部署) the key procurement criterion. Any GenAI service faces "dual filing" (双备案 — algorithm plus model filing), an active enforcement focus per a JunHe alert (Apr 28 2026), plus PIPL obligations that make stores of personal memories regulated data. This is why MemOS ships a zero-cloud Lite variant and why Dify's self-hosting is a feature, not a fallback — and it structurally locks out foreign SaaS memory vendors.

Context economics: the price war ate the context premium

Long context stopped being a premium feature in China because efficiency research became pricing policy. DeepSeek's MLA cut KV cache 93.3% (V2 paper, 2024); production sparse attention arrived with V3.2-Exp on Sep 29 2025, with an immediate 50%+ API price cut (verified); V4 (Jun 2026) serves 1M-token context at 27% of V3.2's per-token FLOPs and 10% of its KV cache. Moonshot's Mooncake KV-cache infrastructure is now embedded in vLLM, SGLang and TensorRT-LLM. By mid-2026, 1M-token context ships on open weights from DeepSeek V4, Kimi K3, GLM-5.2 and MiniMax M3.

The Manus precedent: the arbitrage is closed

The Manus arc (verified via Wikipedia, Aug 24 2026) defines the regulatory wall. Butterfly Effect took a $75M Benchmark-led Series B at ~$500M (Apr 2025), moved HQ to Singapore mid-2025, and agreed to a $2–3B Meta acquisition (Dec 2025). China's NDRC blocked the deal on Apr 27 2026; Meta unwound on Jun 15; Manus relaunched independent from Singapore (~100 staff) on Aug 11 2026. Coverage framed the block as barring "Singapore washing" (Asia Times, May 1 2026), with Beijing broadly tightening on AI firms shedding Chinese ties (WaPo, Apr 21 2026) — mirrored on the US side by the codified reverse-CFIUS outbound rules (Jan 2026). The contrast is Genspark: US-domiciled from day one (ex-Baidu founders), $485M Series B at $2.6B (Jun 2026, per headline), marketing "AI's next breakthrough isn't models, it's context." Teams now pick a bloc before their A round; assets cannot cross later.

Implications

  • Whatever Chinese platforms bundle free — memory storage and memory tokens (Bailian, Aug 2026), Coze's 100-entry memory on-ramp (metered at production scale since Oct 15, 2025) — will face global price-to-zero pressure within roughly a year; a US memory vendor should price on retrieval quality and outcomes, never on storage or context length.
  • China is effectively unaddressable for a US context startup (双备案 filing, 信创 procurement, blocked M&A in both directions since Jan–Apr 2026). Do not build a China GTM; treat the separation as protection from the fastest commoditizers.
  • Chinese competition still arrives via open source: MemOS (Apache 2.0, 11k stars), Coze Studio, Dify (153.4k stars) and Mooncake (already inside vLLM/TensorRT-LLM) compete in US developers' stacks regardless of the market wall. Differentiate on trust, compliance and integration depth, not features those repos ship free.
  • MemTensor's Huawei/Honor/SenseTime cap table (Jul 2026) signals memory becoming a device/OS default in China; the analogous US move is early distribution partnerships with platform vendors before they build in-house.
  • The one segment Chinese buyers pay for — on-prem, private-deployment memory (MemOS Lite/Enterprise, iFlytek appliances) — maps directly to US regulated industries; ship a fully-local variant as table stakes.
  • Benchmark discipline: MemOS publishes LoCoMo 88.83 / LongMemEval 89.20 and a 35.24% token-savings claim against named commercial rivals — US memory products will be score-compared whether they participate or not.

The Dec-2024 cache product is being retired into a standard API

Volcano Ark's dedicated 上下文缓存 (Context API) — the product both sweeps surfaced from Dec 2024 — is now marked 待下线 ("pending offline") in Ark's historical docs (page updated Aug 24, 2026; exact shutdown date not published — unknown). Its replacement is caching folded into the Responses API, which extends caching from text-only to multimodal inputs (text, image, video) and tool-call information. The current context-caching guide (updated Aug 24, 2026) describes a two-mode system: implicit caching — automatic, cannot be disabled, no storage fee, hit not guaranteed, minimum 1,024-token cache block, default-on for doubao-seed-2.0-and-later and doubao-seed-code models — and explicit caching (Session cache and prefix cache) — deterministic hits, configurable expiry via expire_at up to a hard cap of 7 days, storage billed hourly from creation, prefix cache minimum 256 tokens. The pattern matches the US trajectory: the standalone "context product" of 2024 had a roughly 18-month half-life before being absorbed into the standard inference API.

What Volcano charges for context today — cache is paid, not free

Ark's model price list (updated Aug 24, 2026) prices cache hits at a consistent ~80% discount to fresh input across the doubao-seed line, plus an hourly storage fee for explicit caches. Input, hit, and output prices all step up by context-length tier (0–32k, 32–128k, 128–256k input) — the "context-interval pricing" Doubao 1.6 pioneered (per InfoQ-CN headline, Jun 12, 2025) is now the standard scheme.

Two nuances matter for the pricing-squeeze argument. Implicit cache storage is explicitly not billed — only the discounted hit price applies — so light multi-turn workloads get caching effectively free. But production agent workloads that need deterministic hits (role-play, fixed instruction templates, long tool chains) must use explicit caching, pay 0.017 yuan/M tokens/hour storage from the moment of creation ("storage fees accrue at cache creation and stop only on deletion or expiry", per the billing notes), and re-create everything after 7 days. Cache hits are also priced per input-length tier, so a 200k-token cached agent context still hits at the top tier.

Coze memory stopped being free in October 2025

The china-market section's "platforms give memory away free" claim leaned on Coze. That is out of date. Per the current 记忆库 (Memory Base) doc, Coze upgraded long-term memory on Oct 15, 2025 into a standalone Memory Base resource — shareable across agents and workflows, isolated per user-UID×channel-ID — and the upgrade reset legacy memory data unrecoverably. OpenViking: the free layer moved to open source

Where ByteDance does give memory away, it is as code, not as hosted service. volcengine/OpenViking — "the context database for AI agents" — was created Jan 5, 2026 and reached 32,914 stars and 2,511 forks by Aug 24, 2026 (GitHub API), with v0.4.16 released Aug 21, 2026, under AGPL-3.0. It unifies agent memory, RAG, and skills as a viking:// virtual filesystem, processes every entry into three tiers (L0 abstract / L1 overview / L2 details) loaded on demand to cut token spend, and asynchronously extracts user preferences and agent experience into long-term memory after each session commit. InfoQ-CN paired it with OpenClaw as an out-of-the-box fix for agents' "long-term memory dilemma" (长期记忆困局, per headline, Mar 17, 2026). Volcano is simultaneously positioning its paid cloud as the agent data layer — "upgrading data infrastructure as the data foundation for Agents" (per 中国日报网 headline, Jun 24, 2026) — with third-party memory vendors such as ClawLake demoing on Volcano's AI roadshow (per CSDN headline, Apr 2, 2026). The AGPL license is the tell: free for self-hosters, viral for competitors who would offer it as a service.

Implications

  • The "memory is free in China" squeeze claim should be restated: free is the on-ramp (100 Coze memories, unbilled implicit cache), but ByteDance meters memory writes, retrievals, storage, and explicit cache storage at production scale — since Oct 15, 2025 on Coze and in Ark's current cache pricing (Aug 24, 2026).
  • Volcano's ~80% cache-hit discount plus 0.017 yuan/M/hr storage puts it in the same economic shape as DeepSeek and Anthropic caching; hosting deepseek-v4-flash hits at 0.1 yuan/M on Ark extends the cache-hit price war inside ByteDance's own cloud (Aug 21–28, 2026 adjustments).
  • Standalone context products have short half-lives: the Dec-2024 Context API is 待下线 within ~20 months, absorbed into the Responses API — a warning for startups whose product is a caching API rather than the data layer beneath it.
  • Implicit caching is default-on and cannot be disabled for seed-2.0+ models — cache economics are becoming part of the base token price, which compresses what a third-party "context optimization" vendor can charge for.
  • Coze's 15-day arrears auto-deletion of memory data is a real enterprise retention risk and a switching-cost lever; memory data portability is the counter-positioning opening for independents.
  • OpenViking (32.9k stars in under 8 months, AGPL-3.0) means memory startups in China compete against a free ByteDance repo and a metered ByteDance cloud at the same time; differentiation has to come from cross-platform portability or evaluation, not storage.

Addendum: China Memory Vendor Identity Check

Two identity questions behind the China vendor table are now resolved. Memories.ai is a US-domiciled company (ex-Meta founders, US lead investor, US counsel) and should not be counted as a China-market memory vendor. The unnamed Hao Jianye (郝建业) startup is MemoraX AI, a Shenzhen "endogenous memory" (内生记忆) company that closed three rounds in six months: a $10M+ seed (Apr 2026) and a several-hundred-million-RMB seed++ (Aug 2026); the middle round's size is unknown.

Memories.ai: US-domiciled, not a China-market player

Memories.ai was founded by Dr. Shawn Shen, a former Meta Reality Labs research scientist with a Cambridge PhD, and Enmin (Ben) Zhou, a former Meta machine-learning engineer. Its $8M seed (announced Jul 24, 2025; oversubscribed from a $4M target; 15 employees at the time) was led by Susa Ventures, joined by Samsung Next, Fusion Fund, Crane Ventures, Seedcamp and Creator Ventures, per TechCrunch. By Mar 16, 2026 the company had raised $16M total across the seed and an $8M extension (also reported by Dealroom, per headline, Jun 23, 2026), and was shipping a Large Visual Memory Model with Qualcomm and Nvidia partnerships and the LUCI wearable, per TechCrunch.

No fetched source names an HQ city — the company site says only that the work "began at Cambridge" — but three independent signals point to US domicile: Tech in Asia headlined the round "Samsung backs $8m seed round of US ex-Meta team AI startup" (per headline, Jul 25, 2025); US law firm Wilson Sonsini acted as company counsel on the seed (per headline, Jul 24, 2025); and the cap table is US/EU/Korean-corporate with no Chinese investor reported. Founders of Chinese origin and Chinese-press coverage do not make it a China-market vendor: it sells through Qualcomm/Qualcomm-class OEM channels, not into the China stack.

MemoraX AI: the Hao Jianye company, named and dated

The mystery company is MemoraX AI, based in Shenzhen (深圳). Chinese tech press identifies the founder as an ex-Huawei senior figure ("华为大牛" / "前华为高管"), and 36Kr names him directly: "给大模型做记忆，郝建业半年融三轮" — Hao Jianye (郝建业), building memory for large models, three rounds in half a year (per headline, Aug 11, 2026). Its official sites — memorax.ai ("the memory layer for long-horizon intelligence") and memorax.net (reinforcement-learning-based memory; per Bing snippets, Aug 24, 2026) — match Hao's reinforcement-learning profile.
